🌿 Adventure Kit

for Pip and Bramble

🎨 Colour Pip and Bramble

Pip is sitting on her branch, watching Bramble gather berries for winter. Colour the leaves green, the berries red, and the sky a soft blue. Imagine the warm afternoon light settling around them.

🌿 A Small Act of Kindness

Think of someone you care about — a friend, a sibling, or a grown-up. What is one small thing you can do for them today? It does not have to be big. It can be as simple as sharing a smile, handing them something they need, or just sitting beside them quietly.

Draw or tell someone about it.

💭 A Question to Wonder About

“When has someone noticed something you needed, without you having to ask?”

Let your thoughts wander — there is no wrong answer.

🕊️ A Quiet Certificate

“I know that kindness is not about doing everything for someone. It is about noticing what they need — and offering what I can.”

Write your name and the date — and keep it as a reminder that small acts of care are a quiet kind of love.

🌿 A quiet invitation to notice — for little ones and their grown-ups.
