🍂 Adventure Kit

for The Leaf That Took a Nap

🎨 Colour the Leaf, Tree, and Earth

Imagine what it feels like to rest — to stop, to settle, to trust the ground beneath you.

🍂 A Quiet Rest

Find a quiet spot — indoors or outside — and rest for a few moments. Close your eyes. Listen to the sounds around you. What do you hear? What do you feel?

(There is space for your reflections on the PDF version.)

💭 A Question to Wonder About

“What does it feel like to rest — not to sleep, but to simply stop and be still?”

Let your thoughts settle — there is no wrong answer.

🍃 A Quiet Certificate

“I know how to rest — and that is a kind of strength.”

Write your name and the date — and keep it as a reminder that rest is not giving up. It is making space for what comes next.

🌙 A quiet invitation to notice rest — for little ones and their grown-ups.
