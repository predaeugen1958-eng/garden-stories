Stories

little seeds waiting to be read

🌱 For Little Sprouts (2–5 years)

  • The Little Seed Who Didn’t Know It Was a Tree
    A seed lies in the dark, beneath an old oak. It dreams of something it cannot name.
  • The Cloud That Wanted to Stay
    A cloud drifts over a small garden between two hills. The wind says keep moving. The cloud does not want to.
  • The Leaf That Took a Nap
    A tired leaf lets go of the branch and falls. It lands softly. It sleeps.
  • The Moon’s Visit to the Garden
    Every night, the moon looks down at a small garden. One night, she decides to visit.
  • Pip and Bramble
    A bird on a branch. A hedgehog gathering berries. Neither of them says a word about what they are doing.
  • Tyson and Dino
    Tyson loves dinosaurs more than anything. One morning, his parents call him into the garden.

🌿 For Growing Hearts (5–8 years)

  • The Pebble Who Wanted to Roll
    A round pebble longs to roll. The river has other plans.
  • The Kite That Wouldn’t Let Go
    A child makes a kite and carries it up the hill. The wind is warm and steady, the kind that seems to be waiting for something.
  • The Little Mouse Who Listened
    A squirrel is lost in the forest. A mouse who notices everything is sitting at the base of an old oak.
  • The Little Girl Who Visited the Panda Family
    A girl with hair as dark as night follows a bird she has never heard before — and finds a house made of bamboo.
  • The Duck Who Thought He Was a Chicken
    A duckling hatches in a hen’s nest. He tries to cluck. It comes out wrong.

🌳 For Curious Minds (8–11 years)

  • The Last Feather
    A girl finds a silver feather on a mossy stone. Her grandmother says it belongs to a bird that may not exist.
  • The Fox Who Listened to the Forest
    A young fox hears a sound no one else notices — a thread pulling through the air.
  • The Badger and the Magpie
    A badger keeps to the earth. A magpie keeps to the sky. Winter comes early that year.
  • The Path That Was Not There
    Lena walks the same path every morning. One day, there is a gap in the trees she has never seen before.
  • Nova and the Stars
    A fox cub is not afraid of the dark. Not exactly. She is afraid of what she cannot see.
  • The Little Bird Who Was Left Behind
    A young quail does not stay still when the hunter comes. His mother leads the dog away from him.
  • The Second Opinion
    A village doctor has tried everything he knows. In the corner of his clinic, a small machine waits.
  • The Old Man and Ada
    An old man lives alone in a quiet house. One day, his son brings a companion android named Ada.

🌙 Moonlit Stories (11+)

  • The Moon That Held a Promise
    On the night of the Mid-Autumn Festival, a scholar looks up and sees a figure sitting on the edge of the moon.
  • The Library of Unspoken Lives
    A girl finds a door between two shops. Behind it are books that contain the lives she might have lived.
  • The River That Remembered
    A girl has lived her whole life beside a river. One evening, at fifteen, she sees something she has never noticed before.
  • The Jar That Was Never Empty
    A jar of pickles sits on the lowest shelf. It is older than Grandmother.
  • The Last Rocket
    On the centenary of Hiroshima, the last nuclear weapons on Earth begin their journey toward the Sun.
  • The Window
    A ship approaches the speed of light. Ahead, the universe folds into a single point. Behind, there is nothing.
  • The Bowl That Was Always Set
    Every evening, a widow sets two bowls on the table. One for herself. One for a son who has not come home.
