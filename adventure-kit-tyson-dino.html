🌿 Adventure Kit

for Tyson and Dino

🎨 Colour Tyson and Dino

Tyson and Dino are playing in the garden. Colour Tyson's clothes bright and happy. Colour Dino green with a soft, friendly smile. What colour is the sky above them? What colour are the flowers? Let your imagination paint the garden.

🦕 A Dino Hunt

Go on a gentle hunt around your home or garden. Can you find something that is:

  • — Green like Dino?
  • — Round like Dino's tummy?
  • — Soft like Dino's voice?

You do not need to find a dinosaur — just look for things that remind you of one. Draw or tell someone what you find.

🌿 Learn Together

Think of something you would like to learn — how to tie your shoes, count to five, or draw a circle. Now, ask someone to learn it with you, just like Tyson and Dino learn together. It is not about doing it perfectly. It is about doing it together.

💭 A Question to Wonder About

“What does it feel like to have a friend who stays with you, even when you are learning something new?”

Let your thoughts wander — there is no wrong answer.

🕊️ A Quiet Certificate

“I know that the best kind of friend is one who learns beside me — not by teaching, but by being present.”

Write your name and the date — and keep it as a reminder that friendship is a quiet kind of love.

🌿 A quiet invitation to wonder — for little ones and their grown-ups.
