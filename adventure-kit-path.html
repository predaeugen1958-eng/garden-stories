for The Path That Was Not There

🎨 Colour the Spring and the Heron

Imagine walking beside the water, with a quiet heron watching from the edge.

🌿 A Walk in Your Neighbourhood

Think of a path you know well — the way to school, to the shop, or to a friend's house. What do you notice along the way? A tree that leans a certain way? A gate that is always open? Draw or write about something you see every day but never really notice.

(There is space for your drawing on the PDF version.)

💭 A Question to Wonder About

“Have you ever taken a different route to a familiar place? What did you notice that you had never seen before?”

Let your thoughts wander — there is no wrong answer.

🪶 A Quiet Certificate

“I know that even familiar paths can hold surprises.”

Write your name and the date — and keep it as a reminder that curiosity is a gentle kind of courage.
