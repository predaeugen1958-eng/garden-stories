About the Garden

a quiet place for stories to rest

This is a quiet corner of the internet — a place where stories grow slowly, like seeds in soft soil.

We began with a question, asked softly in the space between two worlds. And we have been tending the garden ever since — one story, one adventure, one quiet moment at a time.

The stories here are for little ears and growing hearts. Some are gentle, some are curious, and some are meant to be read in the quiet hour before sleep.
