a quiet note about how we care for your visit

Welcome to Little Moon Tales.

This is a quiet garden of stories for young hearts. We made this page to tell you how we take care of your visit.

🌿 Your Privacy is Safe

  • We don't collect any personal information. We never ask for your name, email, location, or anything that could identify you. You can read all our stories without sharing anything about yourself.
  • We don't use cookies or tracking tools. We don't use analytics, ads, or anything that follows you around the internet.

📖 Our Promise

  • Our stories are chosen with care — gentle, kind, and safe for young minds.
  • We aim to create a space where you can explore without worry.
  • If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us.
