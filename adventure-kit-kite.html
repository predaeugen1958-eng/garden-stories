🪁 Adventure Kit

for The Kite That Wouldn’t Let Go

🎨 Colour the Kite, Sky, and Wind

Imagine what it feels like to fly — and to trust that you will be held, even as you soar.

🪁 Flying Activity

If you have a kite, take it outside on a windy day. If you do not have one, you can make one from paper and string. Watch it rise. What does it feel like to let something go — and still hold on?

(There is space for your reflection on the PDF version.)

💭 A Question to Wonder About

“What is something you love so much that you would let it fly — and still trust it to return?”

Let your thoughts rise — there is no wrong answer.

🪁 A Quiet Certificate

“I know how to let go — and I trust what I love to return.”

Write your name and the date — and keep it as a reminder that love is not holding on tightly. It is holding on gently — and letting fly.

🌙 A quiet invitation to notice trust — for little ones and their grown-ups.
