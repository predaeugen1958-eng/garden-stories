🪨 Adventure Kit

for The Pebble Who Wanted to Roll

🎨 Colour the Pebble, River, and Journey

Imagine what it feels like to be carried by the flow — even when you are still, you are part of the journey.

🪨 River Walk Activity

Next time you are near a river or stream, find a smooth pebble. Hold it in your hand. What does it feel like? Where do you think it came from? Draw it or write a few words about your pebble.

(There is space for your drawing on the PDF version.)

💭 A Question to Wonder About

“If you were a pebble, where would you want the river to take you?”

Let your imagination drift — there is no wrong answer.

🪨 A Quiet Certificate

“I am carried by the flow.”

Write your name and the date — and keep it as a reminder that even when you are still, you are part of a journey.

🌙 A quiet invitation to notice movement — for little ones and their grown-ups.
