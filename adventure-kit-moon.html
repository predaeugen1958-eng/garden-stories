🌙 Adventure Kit

for The Moon’s Visit to the Garden

🎨 Colour the Moon, Garden, and Light

Imagine what it feels like to be visited by the moon — a gentle light in the dark.

🌙 Moonlight Activity

On a clear night, step outside and look at the moon. What does it look like tonight? Is it full, half, or a thin crescent? Draw it below, or write a few words about how it makes you feel.

(There is space for your drawing on the PDF version.)

💭 A Question to Wonder About

“If the moon could visit you every night, what would you tell it?”

Let your thoughts drift — there is no wrong answer.

🌙 A Quiet Certificate

“I am watched over by the moon.”

Write your name and the date — and keep it as a reminder that even when you cannot see it, the moon is always there, watching, listening, returning.

🌙 A quiet invitation to notice the moon — for little ones and their grown-ups.
